Build VELA Collateral Rewards, a working companion to the Vela paper-trading and demo-lending app. Use this brief and the attached starter-kit files. Generate the project in Noah; do not merely produce a landing page.

THE EXPERIENCE
A user connects a wallet, deposits faucet-funded test DOGE into an isolated reward-enabled vault on DogeOS Chikyu testnet, sees collateral rewards accrue from a separately prefunded reserve, and claims actual test DOGE to their wallet. In a separate, clearly labeled DEMO lending panel, the user borrows simulated USD against eligible demo collateral, authorizes paper trading and optionally authorizes automatic repayment from new realized net trading profits. Show debt, interest, repayments, LTV and health factor. A testnet reward receipt and a simulated loan repayment receipt are different records.

Then implement the reward-enabled contract and its tests, followed by verified read integration and the Vela service adapter. Show each stage's tests and unresolved integration requirements. Do not deploy contracts, request secrets or sign wallet transactions during scaffolding. Do not invent deployed addresses, receipts, profits or integrations.

NON-NEGOTIABLE FINANCIAL RULES
1. Testnet yield must have a funded payout source. Use a separate faucet-funded reward reserve, never collateral principal, new deposits or borrowed money. A displayed counter is not a payout. Claim succeeds only after a successful canonical transaction and the required confirmations. Show actual claimed amount and transaction hash.
2. Call the initial rate 5% simple APR, not APY: rewards do not auto-compound. Label it “testnet demo incentives funded by the project; no production yield promised.” DOGE itself is not a staking-yield source. If compounding is added later, show its real mechanism before displaying APY.
3. Existing Vela vaults have no reward checkpoint hooks. Do not attach a current-balance-times-elapsed sidecar to them: it would reward late deposits retroactively. Build a NEW isolated reward-enabled vault/factory candidate. No silent migration. Checkpoint before every deposit, withdrawal, collateral seizure, rate change and reserve top-up. Allocate reserve separately per vault, reserve accrued liabilities, cap accrual at available funded capacity and never backfill unfunded time after a top-up. See src/reward-model.mjs for an executable accounting reference, not deployable contract code.
4. Keep test DOGE rewards and demo USD debt in different ledgers. Claiming DOGE must not silently reduce a USD loan. A real conversion adapter would be a separate future feature. The current showcase is native test-DOGE reward payouts alongside DEMO USD profit sweeps.
5. Reuse Vela's authoritative loan, paper account and repayment services; do not create a second source of balances. If the authenticated Vela service is unavailable, show “integration unavailable” or explicit fixture mode. Noah preview and Vela are different origins: do not copy cookies/tokens or assume cross-origin authentication works. Prefer a reviewed same-origin deployment or a dedicated narrowly scoped integration API.
6. Profit sweeps require explicit, revocable permission: percentage 0–100, expiry, loan identity and optional remaining-balance floor. Sweep only new realized net profits after entry/exit fees and observed funding, excluding unrealized gains, borrowed capital, deposits and rewards. Track already swept profit and losses; never double sweep after retry, restart or reauthorization. Repay accrued interest first, then principal. Reserve open-position margin, pending orders and costs. Stop sweeps when debt reaches zero. Scheduled repayments that can use principal must be separately disclosed and authorized.
7. EVERY account and position, including JP and future accounts, must retain the stricter of existing limits and a $2 fee-inclusive stop-trigger budget. Account size, leverage, strategies and overrides cannot raise it. Round stops inward. Account for partial exits and existing-position reconciliation. Test long and short positions. Never promise a $2 realized-loss maximum: slippage, gaps and funding can exceed it.
8. Show LTV = current debt / current collateral value and health factor = collateral value × liquidation threshold / current debt. Include accrued interest and subtract collateral already committed to sale, without subtracting it again after reconciliation. No debt means health factor is not applicable, not zero. No collateral with debt is a critical condition. Missing/stale prices mean risk is unavailable; block borrowing and new entries rather than treating it as healthy.
9. Initial research-demo parameters are 40% maximum origination LTV, 65% liquidation threshold, 5% annual loan interest, reduce new entry size below HF 1.30 and block new entries below HF 1.15. They are illustrative configuration, not validated production risk settings. Preserve stricter limits. Show a threshold breach separately from an executed liquidation. Current owner-authorized simulated collateral sale is NOT an autonomous on-chain liquidation engine. Do not claim automatic liquidation exists. Record residual bad debt and keep collateral locked until valid settlement; never erase a shortfall.
10. Use decimal strings and integer base units: native test DOGE 18 decimals; demo USD 6 decimals. Show ordinary USD amounts to two decimals, exact units in transaction details. Round spendable prefills down. Show repayment debt, accrued interest and the maximum allowance separately. Vela's full-repay allowance rounds debt upward to cents plus $0.01 for confirmation-time interest; only actual debt is debited up to that maximum.

Keep the existing VELA visual identity: dark, readable dashboard, restrained green accents, clear units and responsive mobile layout. Buttons must perform a real available operation or clearly explain why disabled. Never display fixture success as live execution.

SEVEN REQUIRED CHROME REGRESSIONS
1. Borrowing during a running demo session explicitly pauses new entries and asks for NEW DEMO trading authorization. No misleading “on-chain authorization required” in demo mode. No consent carried silently into financed trading.
2. “AI stopped” shows the reason and the action needed to resume on the main card.
3. Wallet connection times out after 45 seconds, offers Cancel and Retry, releases the network selector, and ignores late approvals from cancelled attempts. Explain that users may still need to dismiss the wallet's own prompt.
4. Negative, zero, blank, malformed, over-precision and over-limit amounts show inline errors and disable Confirm; the server validates independently.
5. Stop success and the status card update together; old polling responses cannot restore “running”.
6. Explain why $10.00 displayed debt can produce a $10.02 maximum repayment authorization. Show interest and the unused buffer treatment.
7. Demo withdrawal prefills do not display 100.009999 as an ordinary dollar amount; use 100.00, preserve the ledger precision and explain fractional-cent remainder.

Browser tests must cover desktop/mobile, every enabled button, cancellation, timeout/retry, rejected signatures, reload while pending, wrong network, stale prices, no fills, and exhausted rewards. Keep fixture tests separate from genuine wallet tests.
