THE SHORT ANSWERS

Keep it simple.

Do I have to trade?

No. Borrowing and repayment work independently. Trading is optional. Enable profit repayment to use a simulated swap and the project test-DOGE reserve.

What happens to my DOGE?

Your test DOGE is held as collateral. Repay the full loan to unlock it, or explicitly use collateral for repayment. This test market has no automatic liquidation.

What is available now?

The new wallet-connected app is available. The DOGE lending pool is active: deposit collateral, borrow up to 70% and withdraw to your wallet. Trades and USDC-to-DOGE swaps are simulated. Opt-in loan repayments are paid in test DOGE from a separate funded project reserve. Existing vaults, rewards and demo history remain available in the app.
