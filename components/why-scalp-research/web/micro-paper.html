Open inventory and execution uncertainty

Fixed protocol and limitations
  • Maker queue is estimated from displayed size; public prints consume that queue before a fill. Cancellations do not advance the queue.
  • Order and cancellation delays are separate. Partial fills, finite depth, post-only rejects and bounded taker slippage are modeled. The 250 ms case is a reference only.
  • Real or injected feed gaps retain inventory. Possible offline fills remain unresolved; that arm blocks new entries for the rest of the session.
  • Funding, liquidation, hidden liquidity and the strategy’s market impact are not simulated. Book source and arrival times are recorded. Modeled fees are assumptions, not a claim about a specific live account.
  • Baselines use source-derived existing rules with shared $100 entry sizing, $300 commitment cap and a $3 session-loss brake. They are counterfactual comparisons, not actual account P&L.
  • Seven days and at least 200 closed entries are review gates, not statistical proof. A smaller sample, correlated fills or unresolved execution gaps cannot establish an edge.
  • No tuning during the frozen window. The experiment does not promote itself to live.
