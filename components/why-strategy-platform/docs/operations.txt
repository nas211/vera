WHY strategy pipeline — deployed 2026-10-05

Runtime: /opt/why-strategy-platform
Development and deployment evidence: /root/why-strategy-platform
Existing WY-REVERT and WY-PULLBACK signal sources are unchanged.

Active processes
- why-strategy-hypotheses: reads the existing AI lab's candidate proposals and
  exports only bounded strategy specifications. No extra model spend or keys.
- why-strategy-research: screens 2,160 initial parameter combinations plus AI
  proposals. Adds up to 256 new combinations per hour when the queue is below
  1,000, up to a 20,000-candidate registry. CPU capped at 30% of one core.
- why-strategy-paper: independent simulated books for up to 200 candidates,
  at most two simultaneous positions per candidate. CPU capped at 20% of a core.
- why-yellow-customer: reads current eligible signals after processing core
  signals and executes through its existing per-customer ledger and protection.

Research model
Five price-based feature families: momentum, breakout, mean reversion, range
rejection, acceleration. The numerical parameter generator is not an LLM.
The existing AI lab supplies genuine model-created hypotheses continuously;
its own model budget and five-family vocabulary still apply. This release does
not autonomously browse the web or execute arbitrary downloaded strategies.
The six original GitHub/Freqtrade shadow bots remain a separate experiment;
their results do not automatically authorise trading on Yellow.

Historical screening
Uses completed, covered Yellow mark candles. Rejects gaps and incomplete bars.
Signals use causal features and enter on the next bar; ambiguous bars stop first.
60% chronological training and 40% validation. Requires ten historical training
trades and three validation trades, positive net result in both. Equivalent
validation trade paths are deduplicated. This is exploratory repeated screening,
not an independently sealed final test. History presently spans only the local
recorder's available days. New screening datasets are archived in state/datasets.
Execution estimates: 1.5 bps per side, 2 bps adverse slippage per side, and a
3 bps/8h funding reserve. These are modeling assumptions, not actual fills.

Forward paper and promotion
Only entries after the candidate's paper admission count. Each strategy has its
own ledger and needs ten completed, valid paper trades with positive net P&L.
No minimum day count, profit-factor threshold, or best-trade-removal condition.
Known data gaps invalidate a simulated trade as promotion evidence. Open losses
are included in eligibility. Stops, targets and maximum holding time are applied.
Paper loss budget is $20 at a modeled $200 notional; breached candidates retire.
Paper uses sampled Yellow marks with modeled spread/slippage, not exchange fills.
Results across independent candidate books are not a single achievable portfolio.

Live integration
Only active, unpaused Yellow customer accounts qualify. WEEX and other venues
need their own evidence and integration. No strategy skips exchange readiness,
market freshness, protection checks, available-margin checks, or existing limits.
The live switch is enabled in config.json; this authorises future qualified
signals. Enabling it does not force a trade or fabricate ten forward results.

Core priority and ownership
- Core signals are processed first; AI signals wait 20 seconds after bar close.
- The core producer must have a fresh snapshot; its open markets are reserved.
- Pending orders reserve both a market and exposure before sending to the venue.
- A core signal may cancel an unfilled AI entry only with confirmed cancellation.
- An already-filled AI position retains ownership and its own exits; the core
  cannot forcibly replace it or close it using an unrelated journal timeout.
- Two opposing AI/core positions cannot intentionally be opened on the same coin.
- After a restart, strategy TP/SL/timeout are recoverable from its immutable name,
  so protection does not depend on the research worker being available.

Customer AI risk limits (owner confirmed 2026-10-05)
All AI positions together: 10% of current exchange account equity in position
notional, not leveraged margin. Maximum two AI positions; existing total slot and
available-margin limits remain. Pending orders count, and exposure uses the higher
of entry/current mark for open positions. Entry sizing reserves 1% slippage room;
market gaps and price changes can still exceed the checked amount after execution.
Start with the venue's minimum valid lot. If it cannot fit, skip it.
After twenty profitable completed live trades for that strategy/account, permit
one doubling of the minimum-lot size, still inside the combined 10% cap.
No unlimited growth or automatic increase of customer account limits.
Per-strategy/account loss budget: three times the initial filled trade's planned
stop loss plus modeled costs. Mark-to-market losses count. Hitting it persistently
blocks new entries for that strategy/account; existing positions retain protection
and timeouts. Paper observation continues. Resetting a live pause is not automated
in this release. Unconfirmed closed-trade P&L blocks further AI entries.

Disable new AI entries
Set live.enabled=false in /opt/why-strategy-platform/config.json atomically.
The gateway reads it each poll; no customer-service restart is required.
Keep Yellow customer execution running to reconcile/manage already-open positions.
Stopping the paper worker also makes its signal file expire after 15 seconds,
but the explicit config switch is the normal operational control.

Rollback
First disable new AI entries. Existing AI positions must remain managed until flat.
Backups and hashes for the six execution integration files are in
review/deployment. Do not remove AI geometry/timeout support while AI positions
or unresolved AI orders remain. The core strategy generator was never edited.
