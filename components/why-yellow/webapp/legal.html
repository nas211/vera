1. What WHY is (and is not)

WHY Trading Bot ("WHY", "we", "us") is a product operated by Modevas FZC, a company registered in the United Arab Emirates. It is software that connects to your account on the Yellow exchange ("Yellow") through an API key you provide, and places trades according to its automated strategy.

  • WHY is non-custodial. Your funds stay in your Yellow account at all times. We never hold, receive, or move your money.
  • WHY is not a broker, exchange, custodian, or investment adviser, and is not registered as any of these. We do not provide financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.
  • WHY is independent of Yellow. Yellow is a third party we integrate with; we do not control it and are not responsible for its availability, pricing, execution, or conduct.

2. Your API keys and permissions

To let WHY trade for you, you create an API key inside Yellow and paste it into WHY.

  • You must create a dedicated API key for WHY with trading and read permissions only.
  • Withdrawal and transfer permissions must be disabled. A key that can withdraw or transfer funds is rejected automatically, and there is no override. This is a safety limit, not a guarantee.
  • Because Yellow does not always expose a key's permissions to us, in some cases WHY cannot automatically confirm that withdrawal is disabled. In those cases your account stays in Pending Yellow verification and will not trade until the permissions are confirmed.
  • You are solely responsible for the permissions on the key you provide. Setting the correct permissions on Yellow is your action, on Yellow's platform, which we do not control.

3. How we store your credentials

  • Your API key and secret are transmitted once over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • They are stored in an encrypted server-side secrets vault (Supabase Vault, using authenticated encryption). Our application database stores only a masked identifier, never the plaintext secret.
  • After setup the secret is never redisplayed. Nothing in the dashboard can retrieve it; you will only ever see a masked form such as xxxx••••xxxx.
  • When you disconnect, the stored credential is deleted.
  • No security measure is perfect. We take reasonable steps to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Privacy — what we collect and why

We collect the minimum needed to run the service:

  • Your email address — to create and secure your account and send account-related email (for example, email confirmation).
  • Your Yellow API credentials — stored encrypted as described above, used only to read your account and place the trades WHY is configured to place.
  • Your trading settings and activity within WHY — such as position size, status, and the orders WHY generates, so the service can operate and show you your history.

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (for example our hosting, database, and email-delivery providers), and only as needed to provide it. Account-confirmation and other transactional emails may be delivered through a third-party email provider on our behalf.
